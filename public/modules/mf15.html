Engagement Depth

DAU/MAU, L7, stickiness — measuring how deeply users engage

DAU is 2 million and growing. Leadership is thrilled. But DAU/MAU is 0.12 — meaning the average user opens the app about 3-4 days per month. The product has a large base of one-time-a-week visitors who inflate MAU and make DAU look healthy, but most users are not forming a habit. Engagement depth metrics like DAU/MAU (stickiness), Lness curves (what fraction of MAU was active on exactly L of the last 28 days), and power user concentration (top 10% share of total sessions) reveal whether growth is building a habit or inflating a vanity number.

How this connects to experimentation: When an A/B test increases DAU, the follow-up question is: did it add new light users (breadth) or did it make existing users come back more often (depth)? If the DAU lift is all breadth, retention may not improve. If it is all depth, you may be over-serving power users at the expense of activation. Decomposing engagement is as important as decomposing a funnel.

Key takeaways

  • Rising DAU with a DAU/MAU of 0.12 means a base of weekly visitors, not habit formation.
  • Depth metrics — stickiness, Lness curves, power-user concentration — reveal habit vs vanity.
  • After a DAU lift, ask: breadth (new light users) or depth (existing users returning more)?
