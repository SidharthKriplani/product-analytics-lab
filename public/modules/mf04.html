Metric Decomposition

Breaking a top-line number into its structural drivers

DAU is down 15% and the growth team wants to invest in re-engagement campaigns while the product team thinks onboarding is broken. Both could be right — or neither. DAU = New + Retained + Resurrected. Until you break it into those three components, you cannot tell whether you have an acquisition problem, a retention problem, or a re-engagement problem. And those three problems have completely different owners, timelines, and fixes.

How this connects to experimentation: In RCA, you decompose the metric before diagnosing cause. In experiment design, you decompose the metric to pick the most sensitive sub-metric as your primary. A 1% lift on DAU is hard to detect; a 3% lift on D7 retention for new users is much easier.

Key takeaways

  • DAU = New + Retained + Resurrected — three problems with different owners, timelines, and fixes.
  • Decompose before diagnosing, or you cannot tell acquisition from retention from re-engagement.
  • In experiments, decompose to pick the most sensitive sub-metric as your primary.
