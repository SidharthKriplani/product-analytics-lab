Synthetic Control

Building a data-driven counterfactual from donor units

You launched in Germany and want to measure the causal impact, but you have no control market — every other market is different in some way. So you build one: a weighted combination of France, Netherlands, and Spain that closely tracks Germany's pre-launch trend. That composite is the synthetic control. A synthetic control is only as good as its pre-period fit — if the donor units don't track the treated unit before the intervention, any post-period gap is uninterpretable. The donor units must also themselves be untreated.

How this connects to experimentation: Used for single-unit interventions (one market, one country, one city) where you can't randomize. Common in geo-based product experiments and policy evaluation.

Key takeaways

  • Build a counterfactual as a weighted blend of donor units that tracks the treated unit pre-launch.
  • Only as good as its pre-period fit — a poor fit makes the post-period gap uninterpretable.
  • Donor units must themselves be untreated.
