## The LinkedIn Content Playbook

Three post types, rotating weekly. One post per week minimum — even during job hunting.

### Type 1 — The Judgment Challenge
Post a real PAL/MSL scenario (stripped of branding if preferred). Ask "What would you do?" Give the audience 24 hours to comment. Then post the expert debrief as a follow-up.

Why it works: two-post engagement loop, every commenter is an implicit referral, trains the audience to associate Avinash with judgment (not question banks).

### Type 2 — The India Insider
Takes on India-specific interview patterns at Swiggy, Zepto, Flipkart, Meesho, PhonePe, etc. Content that Amney literally cannot write because he doesn't have this knowledge.

Why it works: zero competition, high relevance to a large and concentrated audience, directly maps to PAL company tracks (product is content marketing).

### Type 3 — The Builder Perspective
Meta-narrative about building three platforms simultaneously. "Here's what I've learned about how people actually learn." "Why I built a suite instead of a single product." "What 3 platforms taught me about the data career arc."

Why it works: systems-level thinking is rare and commands a different kind of follower — practitioners who want depth, not just tips. This is the Top Voice angle.
